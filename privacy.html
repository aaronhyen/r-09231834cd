Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

This app is used only by its owner to connect the owner's own Google Nest thermostat to a self-hosted Home Assistant server on the owner's home network.

  • Data accessed: thermostat status and settings (temperature, humidity, mode, setpoints) through Google's Smart Device Management API, only for devices the owner authorizes.
  • Use: displaying and controlling the thermostat in the owner's Home Assistant. Data stays on the owner's own server.
  • Sharing: none. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
